Meet it where you work

A useful partner, not another tab.

Copilot capabilities can vary by plan, editor, and enabled settings. These examples show common ways developers work with it.

01 / WRITE

Inline suggestions

Get code completions in context as you type, then accept, adjust, or ignore them.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Chat with context

Ask for explanations, tests, or help debugging without leaving your editor.

03 / DELEGATE

Agentic workflows

Describe a multi-step change and review the plan and edits as they take shape.

04 / IMPROVE

Code review

Surface potential issues and consider suggested fixes before you share a change.
