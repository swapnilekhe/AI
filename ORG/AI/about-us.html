About

Good food,
no small talk.

A small kitchen with strong opinions about butter, sourdough, and eating well.

We believe in the beauty of simple ingredients and thoughtful preparation. Every dish is crafted with care, using locally sourced produce and time-honored techniques passed down through generations. Our mission is to bring warmth and nourishment to your table.
